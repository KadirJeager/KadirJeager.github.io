Beta testing

Thanks for helping test my Android apps. Testing is open to everyone; it takes three steps with the same Google account.

  1. Join the tester group: groups.google.com/g/closebetatesters (press Join group).
  2. Open the app's tester sign-up link below and press Become a tester.
  3. Install the app from Google Play with the link below and keep it installed. Feedback is welcome by email.

Your Launcher: Custom & Smart

A home screen app (launcher) that can look like any launcher you like.

  • Tester sign-up: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.yourlauncher.customsmart
  • Google Play: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yourlauncher.customsmart

Türkçe
